## Scope

This policy covers **ePointer** (屏幕电子教鞭 / 屏幕電子教鞭), a desktop annotation
utility published by OEApp for Windows 10/11 and macOS 13+.

## What ePointer collects

ePointer is designed to run entirely on your device.

- **No account is required.** You can use ePointer without registering or signing in.
- **No personal data is uploaded.** Strokes, annotations, board pages and exported
  files are stored locally on your computer. ePointer does not transmit them to us.
- **No screen content is uploaded.** ePointer renders an overlay on top of other apps
  on your own machine; it does not capture, record or send your screen to any server.

## Purchases

The free download is distributed through the Microsoft Store and the Mac App Store.
Optional in-app purchases (Basic version, Full version) are processed by those
platforms. We receive only the aggregated sales reporting those stores provide to
developers; we never receive your payment card details.
